Our story

Rooted in Bidar, reaching the world.

Bharat Traders was founded by Ranaveer, who grew up in Bidar, North Karnataka. He studied Bioelectronics Engineering at REVA University, Bengaluru, and trained in Java Full Stack development before choosing to build an agri-export company.

The company supplies bananas, rice, fruit and pulses to Gulf markets, and aims to help address development gaps in North Karnataka.

Logistics

From farm to destination port

  1. Farm sourcing

    We work directly with farmers and check quality before harvest.

  2. Grading & packing

    Produce is sorted, graded and packed to export standards, with cold-chain handling.

  3. Documentation

    We handle phytosanitary, APEDA, customs and certificate of origin paperwork.

  4. Shipping

    Shipments leave from Bangalore or JNPT for Gulf destinations.
